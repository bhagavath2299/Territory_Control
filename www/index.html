How it plays

Leave your land and your car pays out a cable from the reel on its tow hitch, staked to the ground as you go. Come back to your land to claim what the cable encloses: the cable pulls up into a new barricade. If a rival drives over your cable while you are outside your land, it snaps there, a spark runs along it and your car goes up. You come back a few seconds later on a fresh patch.

Loop your land round a structure to own it. Gun Armory: a rotary gun (hold FIRE). Missile Silo: two missiles (keep a rival in front of you to lock on, then tap MISSILE). Artillery Battery: tap SHELL for three shells on the locked rival or ahead of you; red rings show where they land. Demolition Depot: tap CHARGE to drop a charge that blows 2 s later. Shells and charges blow craters: that ground is gone for good, nobody can hold it, and a cable through it is cut. Defense Works: each land you claim puts turrets on up to two new barricade towers (six at most).

Every territory has a barricade. Yours opens for you (a rival right behind you can slip through). To ram a rival's open: hit it square and fast; a drift boost hits 1.6x harder, a heavier car harder, and a smaller player hits up to 2.5x harder (the underdog). Towers are 2.5x stronger: go between them. A section still rising is weak. The bigger a territory, the thinner its barricade. Its owner close by repairs it fast. The readout over your speed says what it takes: BREAK, or the speed you need.
